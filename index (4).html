How it works

A screening visit in four steps

ThermalSense is designed as an adjunctive triage tool. It helps decide who should be referred for diagnostic imaging; it does not replace mammography.

  1. Capture

    A trained operator takes thermal images with a portable FLIR ONE Edge Pro camera. No radiation, no compression.

  2. Analyze

    An AI model running on the device looks for abnormal heat patterns associated with breast tumors.

  3. Triage

    The app returns a clear result: routine follow-up, or refer for imaging.

  4. Refer

    Women who are flagged are referred to a provider for diagnostic imaging.

Images are encrypted and stay on the device by default. The operator can share them with another healthcare provider when needed.

Progress

Early results and what comes next

Our model is a ResNet50 fine-tuned on the public DMR-IR breast thermography dataset and optimized to run on the device. These are preliminary research results.
